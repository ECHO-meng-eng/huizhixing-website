  • Prototype review and detail confirmation

Our Production Process

From Materials to Finished Products.

Every step plays an important role in creating quality plush toys, from fabric preparation to final packaging.

01

Cutting

Fabric is cut into the required shapes and sizes according to product patterns.

02

Embroidery

Facial features, logos and decorative details are embroidered onto fabric components.

03

Stitching

Cut fabric pieces are carefully sewn together to form the product structure.

04

Filling

Soft filling is added to achieve the intended shape, softness and overall feel.

05

Quality Check

Products are inspected for appearance, stitching, construction and agreed specifications.

06

International Testing

Applicable product safety tests are arranged according to the destination market and customer requirements.

07

Automatic Packaging

Finished products are packed according to the confirmed packaging specifications and order requirements.

Quality mindset

Details matter.

Product appearance, stitching, components, labels and packaging all contribute to the final result. Quality requirements are discussed and confirmed according to each project.

Specific testing, inspection standards and certifications should be confirmed for each product and order.
